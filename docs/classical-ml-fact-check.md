# Classical ML seed fact check

Reviewed all 39 entries in [the category seed](../data/classic/classic.classical-ml.seed.json) on September 30, 2026.
Sources below are original papers, publisher records, author or institution archives, and project documentation.

Dates identify the publication or public-release milestone explained in `yearAnnotation`, not necessarily the first invention of a whole method family.
A year is kept when a day is not established; conference start dates and January 1 placeholders are not substituted for publication dates.
Naive Bayes, Gaussian Process and Multi-Layer Perceptron have no uniquely established release date at the scope of these entries, so their dates are null and they are omitted from the generated Timeline catalog.
Countries refer to the affiliations or development locations of the credited work, not author nationality.
Difficulty pools, IDs and entry order are game configuration and were preserved.

All entries describe methods, architectures or training libraries rather than released pretrained checkpoints, so `weightAvailability` is `not-applicable`.
Tabular input and prediction output spelling is consistent; decomposition outputs are features or embeddings, and HMM/CRF input and output are sequences.

For Decision Tree, the provenance is specifically CART, consistent with the existing alias and the listed scikit-learn implementation; it is not a claim that all decision trees originated in 1984.
Agglomerative Clustering and Spectral Clustering retain explicitly named publication milestones rather than claiming a single invention of their entire families.
The NMF milestone is the 1999 Lee-Seung paper, with [Paatero and Tapper's earlier 1994 work](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/env.3170050203) acknowledged in the annotation.
For QDA, [UCL's catalog of Smith's actual 1947 offprint](https://archives.ucl.ac.uk/calmview/Record.aspx?id=MS+ADD+426%2FC%2F1%2F1%2F11&src=CalmView.Catalog) supports keeping 1947 despite the [publisher's conflicting volume metadata](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1469-1809.1946.tb02368.x).
LightGBM's [2017 paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/file/6449f44a102fde848669bdd9eb6b76fa-Paper.pdf) credits collaborating institutions in China and the United States.
The MLP annotation distinguishes Rosenblatt's architecture work from [Rumelhart, Hinton and Williams's 1986 training paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/323533a0).
